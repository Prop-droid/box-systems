## Verdict

Platform plumbing is functional and all monitored feeds are currently fresh, but the codebase is carrying a meaningful debt load: the signals backbone (`/api/research/today`) has a bare-500 failure path, two high-traffic routes are missing Next.js declarations that could cause silent build-cache staleness, and four high-severity bugs from last week's review have had zero commits against them in 7 days. The single highest-value fix is wrapping `/api/research/today`'s GET in a try/catch with a proper 502 — it is the data spine for the entire Signals tab, and right now any transient fs or BQ error cascades into retried, BQ-billed empty-state renders with no operator visibility.
