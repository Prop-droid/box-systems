## Verdict

The feedback machine is the biggest structural problem: the creative verdict ledger has never received a write (0 bytes, 10 weeks), the synth cron cannot report its own failures, and the override ledger has been silent for 54 days — the entire feedback → rules → threshold loop is effectively dead with no alarms.
