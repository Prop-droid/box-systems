## Creative-strategy insights (worth internalizing for SHA)

1. **Format fatigue is a real CPA driver** — @adswithcami (`DbSuez1sYGE`).
   Running B-roll+VO, UGC, and native and still seeing CPAs climb = the *format*
   is fatigued, not just the creative. Rotate format families, not only hooks.
   → Aligns with your `fatigue-sentinel` cron and the 16-family image taxonomy.

2. **Entertainment-first is the 2026 bar** — @adswithcami (`DY5I7BGDBy0`).
   "The most important thing when making ads in 2026 is to make them entertaining
   — you're competing with million-follower creators, not other ads."
   → This is already your canon (`feedback_entertainment_first_creative`); good
   external confirmation. Keep pitching one parody/comedy option per batch.

3. **Shock advertising rarely pays** — @daradenney (`C_3X6rEpQnn`).
   9/10 times high hook-rate shock tactics aren't worth it: attention without
   brand fit burns trust and CAC. Useful counterweight when a "big swing" hook
   tempts. → Candidate note for the saturation/exploration gate: shock ≠ pattern
   interrupt; judge by brand-safe attention, not raw hook rate.

4. **AI-ad craft reference** — @ilian.zut (`DQ7bmvXjIAF`, `DQzMOTfkd8V`).
   Midjourney + Veo3 "fashion ad test" spots for Miu Miu / Diesel. Not strategy,
