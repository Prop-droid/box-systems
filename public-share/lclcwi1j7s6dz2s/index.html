SHA ad performance: what to change in our system (2026-10-01)

Sources: 4 research passes in raw/ (1 internal BQ audit, 2 Meta operators, 3 AI tools/open source, 4 Reddit/X). BQ headline numbers re-verified independently. Window: 26 weeks, 2026-03-30 to 2026-09-27, SHA Meta.

The one-line answer

We don't have a creative-volume problem or a tooling problem. We have a selection-and-allocation problem: too many near-identical variants that never get a read, winners funded after they've stopped winning, and our findings never reach the person who moves budget.

Where the money leaks (BQ, verified)

  • 26w Meta: $4.07M spend, $3.33M revenue, ROAS 0.817. First-order break-even is ~1.54, so first-order contribution is about -$1.95M. Paid DTC CM ran $0.84M worse than plan (Apr-Sep), mostly in the over-budget months (Apr 150%, Jul 159%).
  • 69% of variations launched Apr-Aug (3,513 of 5,044) got under $100 in their first 28 days. Two-thirds of the team's output never gets a real test.
  • Money hit rate: 103 variations (2.0%) reached $500+ at ROAS 1.0+ in 28 days. Only 14 (0.3%) cleared 1.54.
  • Winners decay fast and get funded late. Spend on those 103 hits by age: wk 0-1 $77K at 1.38, wk 2-3 $70K at 1.12, wk 4-7 $60K at 0.95, wk 8+ $190K at 0.77. Almost half their money lands after they're below 1.0.
  • SH-16975-3 took $165K at 0.74 after the 2026-08-18 finding said to move budget to sibling -2 (1.19 on $69K). Upper bound on that one miss: ~$73K revenue.
  • Lane yield vs production volume:
  • WL / creator: 1,891 assets (most of any lane), $783K at 0.70, 1.4% hit.
  • Launch tasks: 339 assets, 7.7% hit at 0.99, only 4% of spend.
  • Offer-led statics lead: Scarcity/Urgency 1.04, Direct Offer 0.95. Problem-Solution 0.62 and Curiosity 0.59 still get hundreds of launches.
  • Video 59% of spend at 0.78 (CPM $38); image 0.87 (CPM $30).
  • LPs: target ~1.0 and glp-2 1.35 (now off) beat trw-gifts (0.85, 42% of spend with it2-vp). swap burned $317K at 0.68. 30% of spend has no identifiable LP.
  • LTV is unknown at creative level: 92% of orders are subscriptions, but the subscription table has no creative key. Directional cohort read: rebills add ~0.75x first-order revenue, which would put the real (LTV) break-even somewhere around 0.85-1.0 first-order ROAS, not 1.54. Needs finance to confirm before any threshold is built on it.

Where our system leaks

  • Proposals don't reach action: iteration-suggestions has had 0 "go" replies since 07-29. Weekly-report kill/scale calls go to Tomas, who isn't the media buyer, and reach nobody else. Verdict ledger: 5 records ever.
  • Nothing checks outcomes: the planned brief-vs-result check isn't built, and its ai-brief tag was banned 2026-09-03.
  • Broken sensors:
  • Weekly report runs before Sunday US data lands (week of 9/21 reported $139K vs $162K actual, -14%).
  • fatigue-sentinel scans all eJam brands on a partial-day window (watches 5-10 ads; SHA alone had 53 at $100+; false "ROAS 0.00" alerts).
  • iteration posts carry raw {{SH-…}} placeholders; comments-digest failed 09-29; cm-roas-recut stale since 08-07; format-lifecycle dead since 08-03; launch-autofill has no timer.
  • Wrong success metric: the winners archive is built from the media buyer's "winner" label (avg CM-ROAS ~0.70), so briefs learn from the least-unprofitable ads. Two CM-ROAS formulas in use (0.52 vs 0.57). Hook/CTR-led tools run against our own finding that hook rate is inverse to ROAS.
  • Blind spots: video (59% of spend) has 0% ai_angle tags; 30% of spend has no LP.

What others do that we should copy (external, sourced in raw/2-4)

  1. Concepts, not variants. Meta clusters visually similar ads under one Entity ID and delivers one of them. Meta added a per-ad-set Creative Diversity column (Low/Med/High, visual similarity) on 2026-08-26, labelled "estimated and in development". Reports from operators (Marketing Concurrent 09-07, plus 5+ X operators) say different backgrounds or frames around the same product photo still score Low. This is the most likely mechanism behind our 69% no-spend rate. (The "60% similarity = suppressed" number that circulates has no Meta source; don't cite it.)
  2. A concept-level gate, not ad-level pausing. Jones Road lets each concept spend ~$1k before deciding; Ridge runs a hybrid CBO where each concept is its own ad set with a minimum budget and winners absorb the rest (~40% of spend over 60 days). Practitioner rules seen on X: kill under 1.0 ROAS after 1.5x target CAC; iterate anything over 1.8 ROAS on $500.
  3. Statics as the testing engine (several operators at 70% statics; "20 tests for the price of one video"). Matches our data.
  4. Partnership ads have the best external numbers (one Meta lift test: CPA $25.63 vs $39.34). Ours is the opposite (WL 0.70). The likely difference is spread: 1,891 assets across many creators vs a few proven ones. Meta's Creator Marketing Hub (2026-09-15) adds creator discovery by predicted performance.
  5. Measure incrementality before cutting or scaling. Operators run holdouts or Conversion Lift; one r/FacebookAds holdout found Meta claimed 60%+ non-incremental sales. Haus data (vendor) says Meta's Incremental Attribution setting beat standard attribution 1.38x for DTC-only brands (Jul 2026); Loomer is skeptical.
  6. Lock Advantage+ Creative. Since 2026-07-27 Meta auto-rewrites on-image text into up to 8 headline variants, and Common Thread reports the settings re-enabling themselves. Risk to canon claims (26g fiber, 70 cal). Fix: Restricted Words list plus a weekly toggle audit.
  7. Official Meta Ads MCP (open beta since 2026-04-29): read-only scope gives Creative Diversity, opportunity score, anomaly signal, A/B and lift tools that BQ can't. New entities land paused.
  8. Hypothesis registry ("set the bar before you look", keep dismissed bets with reasons), from Parker Brain's open method. No vendor ships it.

Not worth it: buying Motion/Segwise/etc. (we already have the data; labels are the gap), pre-launch scorers as a hard gate (best published model R² 0.21 on ecommerce; shadow mode at most), synthetic persona panels, AI avatars as first-person testimonials (FTC/NAD risk: an avatar "cannot have used a product"), autonomous write-access buying agents (ban reports).

Contradictions worth knowing: external "partnership ads win" vs our WL lane losing; "a winner is an ad live 60-90 days" vs ours fading by week 3; Foxwell/Motion "more volume" vs Loomer/HexClad "fewer, more distinct concepts" (our data sides with fewer, more distinct).

The plan, ranked by $ impact per hour

A. Our lane: production + creative (Tomas decides)

  1. Fewer variants, more distinct concepts. Cap variants per concept (proposal: 3-4), make each new concept visually different (background, format, talent, setting, not just headline). New production KPI: % of variations reaching $100 in 28 days (now ~31%).
  2. Re-weight the brief mix toward what yields: more Launch-type tasks and offer-led statics; fewer Problem-Solution/Curiosity statics; cut WL/creator volume to the creators with proven ROI (Cruva profiles already exist).
  3. Retire the media-buyer "winner" label as our learning source; rebuild winners.jsonl on a money definition (spend floor + CM-ROAS) so skills learn from ads that made money.

B. Our system: fix what's broken (hours, no new tools)

  1. Move sha-weekly-report to after Sunday US data lands; fix fatigue-sentinel (SHA only, full-day windows); resolve {{SH-…}} links; fix comments-digest; re-run cm-roas-recut; one CM-ROAS formula everywhere.
  2. Tag video: Gemini pass over video asset links for angle/formula plus pacing, CTA timing, on-screen text (59% of spend currently blind).
  3. Brief→result check: hypothesis + bar recorded at brief time, verdict auto-filled from BQ at day 28 via clickup_id (100% coverage). Replaces the dead ai-brief plan.

C. Hand to the buying team (they own budget)

  1. Weekly budget-actions digest sent to the media buyer, not Tomas: scale fresh winners (wk 0-3 above bar), cut decayed ones (wk 4+ below bar), sibling swaps like SH-16975-3 to -2. Money-based rules, one table, links.
  2. Concept-level testing structure (Ridge hybrid CBO with minimum ad-set budgets, or a ~$1k-per-concept gate) so every concept gets a read.
  3. Advantage+ Creative: Restricted Words list + weekly toggle audit (compliance).
  4. Read-only Meta Ads MCP grant for us (Creative Diversity per ad set into our weekly report).
  5. One incrementality test (Conversion Lift or geo holdout) and an agreed LTV-adjusted break-even ROAS from finance. With -$1.95M first-order contribution, this decides whether more spend helps at all.
