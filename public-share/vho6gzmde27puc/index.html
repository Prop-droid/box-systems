Weight-Loss Top-of-Funnel Ads — Deep Research

Date: 2026-09-01 Question: what should Shameless run at top of funnel for the weight-loss / calorie-counter buyer (30% of buyers), now that the WL landing page copy is locked? Sources: BigQuery creative_dashboard (SHA, 2026-03-01 to 2026-08-31, prospecting only), Atria pull of 600 competitor ads across 5 brands (2026-09-01), wiki canon, compliance canon, Meta 2026 ad standards.

Verdict

Stop trying to sell weight loss. Sell the swap, and let the arithmetic do the weight-loss work.

Every number we have says the same thing: the moment a Shameless ad names the goal, it gets worse. The moment it shows a comparison, it gets better. The new LP is already built this way ("10 Reasons Why These 70-Calorie Gummies Are The Best Candy Swap"), so the TOF job is message-match, not a new promise.

Three readings that matter:

  1. Explicitly weight-framed creative is the worst-funded and worst-performing family we have. WeightLoss + LowCal together = $35k over six months at 0.36 to 0.40 CM-ROAS, against a 0.572 fiber baseline. That is not "untested." It is tested small and it lost.
  2. Keto is the buried lead. 0.807 CM-ROAS, $66 CPA, on $6.2k. It is the same buyer (macro-aware, calorie-aware) approached through a food identity instead of a body goal. The single best WL-adjacent asset in the account is SH-3908-1 (LastCall FlavorCombo, Keto token, image) at 1.116 CM-ROAS on $19.2k. It is a flavor-and-offer static, not a diet ad.
  3. Swap has the highest hook rate in the set (30.7%) and mediocre CM-ROAS (0.450). Classic hook-rate-inverse-to-ROAS: the swap idea earns attention easily, then the current executions fail to close. That is a copy/LP-match problem, not an angle problem, and the new LP is the fix.

2. What the category is doing right now

Atria pull, 2026-09-01, 600 ads across ColonBroom, Noom, Supergut, SmartSweets, BelliWelli. Variant count = how hard a brand is scaling one piece of copy.

ColonBroom has bet the entire top of funnel on anti-injection flanking. Three clusters launched inside the last 4 days: "Skip The Shot. Keep The Results." (60 video variants), "Skip the needle. Keep the results." (36 image variants), "Skip the injection. 70% OFF" (17 variants). Body copy is a week-by-week diary ("Day 3 ... Week 1 ... Week 2") plus a cost-and-side-effect stack against the drug ($1,000+/month, nausea, 6-month waiting lists, and what happens when you stop). Their mechanism line is the sharpest copy in the pull: "You didn't fail the diet. The diet never addressed what was actually broken."

Supergut owns the trend word. "Fibermaxxing Starts Here" plus a "GLP-1 Daily Support" SKU name, sold as a two-second morning habit ("Before Your Coffee, Do This", 53 days running, 11 variants). They are winning a term that our product outguns 26g to 6g or 7g.

SmartSweets, the direct candy competitor, never says weight. Every unit is a comparative percentage plus a flavor: "87% less sugar, 19g fibre," "91% less sugar than traditional foam candy," "5 Candies, 1 Bag, 4g Sugar." Their longest-running units are lunchbox and share-size retail, not diet.

Noom is a quiz-and-price machine: microdose GLP-1 at $79 to $99, "Medicare Now Covers Your GLP-1," free 5-minute quiz. Not our lane and not our economics.

The gap this leaves: everyone in the weight-loss lane is selling a replacement for the drug. Nobody is selling compatibility with the life. ColonBroom, Supergut and Noom all fight over "instead of the shot." Nobody is talking to the person who is on it, or off it, and just wants something sweet at 9pm that does not cost them the day.

3. The fence

Meta 2026 tightened exactly where weight-loss creative lives:

  • Personal-attributes enforcement now catches implied knowledge, not just explicit. Second-person body framing ("lose your belly fat", and by extension "if you're counting calories") is disapproval bait. (Meta Health & Wellness standards, personal health policy)
  • Before/after and idealized-result imagery: prohibited.
  • No specific loss amounts, no time-bound transformation, no under-18 targeting. (2026 sensitive category summary)
  • Nothing that generates negative self-perception to sell a diet product.

Our own canon adds: no "only" before a nutrient stat, no whole-bag permission framing, no "appetite suppressant," no drug names, no natural/real-fruit language, gut and regularity cleared but treatment verbs and named conditions banned, structure-function disclaimer rides in the ad copy on any benefit-mechanism creative, and craving beats must never be visualized as eating less.

Net effect: the compliant surface for a weight-loss TOF ad is food versus food. Two products, two numbers, no viewer.

4. The concept slate

Candy-first per live lead doctrine: taste and the low number lead, fiber is a late proof point. All hooks written for the 16 to 25 second money zone. Batch rule 3 default plus 2 swings.

Default lane (3 of 5)

1. The Granola Bar Trial Hook: "A granola bar is 190 calories. This whole bag is 70." Format: static plus a 16s product-in-hand video. Equal-number contrast, both sides measured the same way. Why: message-matches the LP block that is already live and substantiated. Zero viewer callout, so it clears personal attributes. Swap hook rate (30.7%) says the comparison earns the click; the LP now closes it. Risk: comparative nutrition number, so the comparator stays exactly as substantiated on the LP.

2. Sixteen Minutes Hook: "That granola bar is a 44 minute walk. This bag is 16." Format: split-frame static or CTV, product B-roll on top per the winner composition. Why: converts calories into effort, which is the weight-loss idea without a weight-loss claim. Already drafted for the LP and awaiting your go, so ad and page ship together. Risk: needs "a 155 lb adult" inline. Keep it attached to the food, never to the viewer. Fallback with no body weight: "almost three times the walk."

3. Nine PM Hook: "9pm. Kitchen's closed. This is what I eat instead of going back in the freezer." Format: UGC talking head, 16 to 25s, taste-conditional open, candy in frame the whole time. Why: the nightly-treat habit CTA is the house default and it is the calorie counter's actual moment of failure. Sells the swap as a routine, not a diet. Risk: keep the visual on the snack drawer, never on a refused meal.

Swings (2 of 5)

4. On The Shot (the reopened asset) Hook: rebuild of SH-6084-1, our 56.9% hook-rate ad, now that the gut and regularity payload is cleared. Format: UGC, first person, symptom language only, structure-function disclaimer in the ad copy. Why: two of the top three hook rates in the entire WL-adjacent set came from this exact open, and it has been off the table since June. Highest-expected-value single test on this list. Risk: no drug names, no equivalence, no named conditions, symptom never diagnosis. Route the final script through compliance before it ships.

5. Skip The Argument Hook: "Everyone's arguing about the shot. I just wanted candy that fits." Format: 20s straight-to-camera, dry delivery, no medical content at all. Why: direct counterprogram to ColonBroom's 113-variant "Skip the Shot" push. They sell replacement of the drug and inherit every claim risk that comes with it. We sell compatibility and carry none. This is the uncontested half of the biggest conversation in the category. Risk: low. It is an opinion, not a claim. Keep it out of mechanism territory.

Two funded plays that are not concepts, just money

6. Fund keto properly. 0.807 CM-ROAS and $66 CPA on $6.2k is the best signal in the account and it is starved. Build a proper keto and macro static batch off the SH-3908-1 flavor-combo template. Keto-Friendly is a permitted cert where true per SKU.

7. Trend-jack fibermaxxing, for fiber-targeted audience sets only. "Fibermaxxing, but it's candy." Supergut is spending 53 days on a word describing a habit our product does better at 26g versus their 6g. Fiber-lead is only allowed against fiber-targeted audiences per doctrine, so this is a segment buy, never broad.

5. What not to run

  • Any second-person body or diet callout. "If you're counting calories" trips the same policy as "lose your belly fat."
  • Before/after, scale shots, transformation testimonials, product beside a fit body.
  • "Lose weight with candy" in any phrasing. Our own data already priced this at 0.401 CM-ROAS.
  • Whole-bag permission framing. Banned June 2026, and it is the DNA of our biggest historical winner, so it will keep resurfacing in drafts. Kill it on sight.
  • "Only 70 calories." Bare stat only.
  • A fiber-first hook on broad traffic. The SH-17215 A/B settled it: same creator, same week, candy opener 1.28 ROAS versus fiber opener 0.68.

6. Recommended test

One ConceptTest batch of 5 against the new WL landing page: concepts 1, 2 and 3 as the defaults, 4 and 5 as the swings. Every cut 16 to 25 seconds, product B-roll filling the top half where the format allows, taste-conditional open, no number until roughly second six, fiber demoted to a closing beat. Read on CM-ROAS at $500 spend per cut, never on hook rate alone.

Parallel: a keto and macro static batch off SH-3908-1, and a fibermaxxing unit restricted to fiber-targeted audience sets.
