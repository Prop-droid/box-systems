How to read this

  1. Rows are landing-page builds, not URLs. One URL can be served by several builds (/trw-gifts/ is three: FrontrowMD, Holiday Sale, Holiday NYNM), and one build can serve several URLs (the FrontrowMD build also sits behind /glp-1/). The build is what the visitor actually saw, so that is the row. The map at the bottom shows every build-to-URL pair.
  2. Contribution = revenue + upsell − COGS − merchant fees − ad spend. CM % = contribution ÷ revenue. Goal Pacing definitions, after ad spend. First-order attribution only, so negative is normal here: subscriptions pay back on rebills. Compare rows against each other, not against zero.
  3. Add to cart on these pages is the click from the page into checkout (checkout views confirm it within 3%). $ / LP view is what a visitor costs. Together they explain CPA: the click costs about the same on every page; the cart rate does not.
