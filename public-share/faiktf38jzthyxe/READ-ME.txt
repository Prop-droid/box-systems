TV PC remote setup (Tailscale + SSH + Claude Code)

1. Plug this stick into the TV PC (Desktop mode).
   Game Mode? Steam button → Power → Switch to Desktop first.
2. Open the stick in Dolphin, right-click on empty space → "Open Terminal Here".
   (Bazzite GNOME: open it in Files, right-click → "Open in Terminal".)
3. Type:  bash pc-setup.sh   and press Enter.

It asks for your PC password once, then shows a Tailscale login link (approve it),
then opens Claude: log in with your Claude subscription, say yes to the two prompts, type /exit.
When it says DONE, write "done" in Discord #dev.
Safe to run again if anything fails halfway.
