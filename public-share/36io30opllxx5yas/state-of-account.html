State of the Account + Testing Plan (S35–S38)

Nine months of data (Dec 2025 – Aug 17, 2026), the current 28-day read, relaunch candidates, and the Aug 24 – Sep 20 test plan. Aug 19, 2026 · v2 · Creative Strategy.

TL;DR

Where the account actually is

  • The account was at break-even exactly once — January (cmROAS 1.0). It collapsed in the April scale-up (spend 2.4×, cm 0.71 → 0.55) and has been flat at 0.57–0.60 for three months. We run ~$120k/week at 0.83 platform ROAS today.
  • Counting subscription rebills, true cmROAS is ≈0.74 vs break-even 1.0. We need ~+35% efficiency, not a miracle.
  • It’s a funnel problem more than a creative problem. Pages lose the sale after creative earns the click; the two biggest fixes need zero new creative (see the LP & Checkout Audit).
  • The only angle lanes that ever cleared 1.0 — Scarcity and Offer — had their budget cut ~65% after Q1. Re-funding them is the single highest-margin decision available.
  • There is a relaunch pool: 15 compliant ads at cm ≥0.85 killed in the March–April restructure, including a $132k lifetime asset at cm 1.12. They died in a cleanup, not on performance.
  • S35–S38 tests angles and personas on fixed LPs so every cell produces a clean read.

Relaunch mechanics

  • One revival ad set, $500–1k per asset, launched as-is (same creative, same LP routing the asset won on — never re-route a winner).
  • Judge on cmROAS vs the current 0.58 baseline after $500 spend. Anything ≥0.75 today is a keeper and a retro-brief candidate; expect prints below lifetime cm — Q1 season is gone.
  • Compliance gate before launch: the two gut-health assets stay out until the words are reworked (device stays, words change). Scan the other eleven for banned phrasing — several predate the June counsel review.
  • Scarcity-heavy pool = double duty: the revival IS the Offer/Scarcity re-funding test the plan already calls for. If the pool prints ≥0.75, the S35 Offer cells get their budget conversation answered with data.
  • Winners feed the retro machine: any asset that holds becomes the body for hook retros (our cheapest slot machine, 71% keep rate).

Video brief rules

Non-negotiables for every S35–S38 video cell

  • Taste-conditional hook (“if you love sour candy → try these”), then stats as proof — candy first, fiber second.
  • Split-frame with product on top; hands and product always in frame.
  • 16–25 seconds. That’s the money zone; most budget currently sits at 36–50s.
  • Compliance gate before the batch ships: top past winners are built on now-banned language. Keep the device, replace the words.
