The logic

Why the four weeks are in this order

Every test has two unknowns available — the angle and the audience — and a clean read requires holding one still. So: S35 puts new personas on angles we already trust (Offer, Testimonial, Bloodsugar) — if a cell over- or under-performs, the persona explains it. S36 inverts that: angles we're unsure about get their fairest possible shot by pairing with the loudest-voice personas. S37 asks whether S35's winners travel — a winning cell could be the angle, the persona, or the specific pairing, and giving the top personas a second angle isolates which. S38 doubles what won and turns the month into the account's first real persona-level margin read. The LP is fixed per cell (mostly target, the best closer) so page quality never contaminates a creative read — it's in each brief, don't change it.

Every brief · non-negotiables

The rules — and why each exists

  • Video: open straight on the taste-conditional hook ("if you love sour candy → try these"), then stats as proof. Candy first, fiber second — the candy-first pivot is the one repositioning that survived the margin recut. (Offer-card cold open dropped from the rules — Aug 19 decision. Offer-wrapped statics and the GLP-1 offer wrapper are unaffected.)
  • Video: split-frame with product on top, hands and product always in frame, 16–25 seconds. That duration band is where the money is; most current budget sits at 36–50s, which is why so much of it underperforms.
  • Compliance gate before every batch ships: no gut-health / prebiotic / digestive claims, no "eat the whole bag" framing — several of our best historical winners are built on now-banned language. Keep the device, replace the words. "26g fiber per bag" is cleared.
  • No 9pm/late-night wording anywhere — that anchor is retired as saturated; rotate scene anchors within each batch so no two briefs in a week share one.
  • Marketing flavor names in copy, never Amazon fruit descriptors.
