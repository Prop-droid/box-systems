Landing Page & Checkout Audit

Server-side funnel data: 28-day close-up (Jul 21 – Aug 17) + nine-month view (Dec 2025 – Aug 2026) + live page teardown of all six LPs and the shared checkout. Aug 19, 2026 · v2 · Creative Strategy.

TL;DR

Five findings, one theme: we lose the sale after the click

  • All six landing pages feed ONE shared checkout page. $547k of $550k spend converges on a single CheckoutChamp page. One fix lifts every LP at once.
  • The leak is precisely located. 71k add-to-carts became only 8.1k checkout starts (11.4%). Once someone starts the form, 78% finish. The problem is the offer-reveal step, not payment.
  • The offer is consistent at 46% — re-verified after a challenge. Visible LP buttons, the checkout and ad copy all say 46%. A dormant “Get 58% Off” block sits hidden in the markup of 5 pages. The real killer is price-reveal shock: no dollar price exists anywhere before the checkout.
  • The six LPs are one listicle template with headline swaps — and two of the swaps are live compliance violations (gut-health claims on legal hold).
  • This is a mobile business with a desktop-grade page. 96% of spend is phone; phone closes 8.7% of carts vs desktop’s 13.1%. The checkout is a 1MB two-in-one page.
  • NEW — the 9-month check says none of this is recent. The start-rate cliff has sat in a 10.7–13.5% band across 12 checkout versions since December. Every iteration improved the finish step; nobody has ever tested the start step.

Measurement

How we’ll know it worked

  • One variable per test. LP/checkout changes run on existing creative; the S35–S38 angle×persona cells route to fixed LPs so reads stay clean.
  • Checkout A/B reads at 300 carts/arm (~2 weeks at current traffic). LP swaps read after 7 days.
  • cmROAS is the only ranking metric. Cart rate and close rate are diagnostics that say where to look, not what won.
  • Success line: gift-page close 7.7% → 9%+ = ~25 orders/week; full target parity = 35–40. Watch sub attach (93%) as the guardrail — a fix that closes more carts but kills attach is a loss.
  • Everything lands in the weekly report automatically — LP rows and the persona×angle matrix come from the same pipeline.
