4-Week Action Plan

Aug 19 – Sep 20, 2026. The full plan with the reasoning attached: what happens, who does it, what "done" means, and why each move earns its slot. Built from the LP & Checkout Audit, the 9-month account deep-dive and the S35–S38 testing plan. Creative detail lives in the creative plan.

The diagnosis

Why the plan looks like this

The account loses the sale after the click, not before it. In the last 28 days, 257,827 LP views became 70,950 add-to-carts (27.5% — healthy), but only 8,082 checkout starts (11.4% — the cliff). Once a buyer starts the form, 78% finish. Nine months of data proves this cliff is structural: it held between 10.7% and 13.5% across twelve different checkout versions. Every version improved the finish rate (63%→79%); none touched the start step, where a buyer meets the first dollar amount, a subscription default and a 4-way pack choice all at once — having never seen a price on any LP.

The second finding: the account defunded its best weapon. Scarcity/Offer is the only creative lane that ever cleared 1.0 at scale ($324k at 1.13 in Q1) — then its budget was cut ~65% in the spring restructure. That same restructure killed 11 compliant ads on pause dates (Mar 27 / Apr 21), not on performance. And the buyer is a 55+ woman on a phone (54% of spend on 55+, 96% mobile) whom we've never deliberately cast for, closing carts at 8.7% on a device where desktop closes 13.1%.

So the plan does three things at once, each on its own track: (1) re-fund what worked — the revival pool is both a relaunch and the Offer/Scarcity budget test; (2) fix the funnel — price on the LP, simpler mobile cart, budget toward pages that close; (3) run S35–S38 — four weeks of angle × persona cells on fixed LPs so the account finally learns who buys what. The tracks don't touch each other's variables, which is what keeps every read clean.

What "it worked" looks like on Sep 20

  • Gift-page cart→order close moves from 7.7% toward 9%+ (~25 extra orders/week; full target parity is worth 35–40). This is the checkout A/B + mobile cart work paying out.
  • At least 3–4 revival keepers ≥0.75 cmROAS at today's baseline — data-backed proof the Offer/Scarcity lane deserves its budget back.
  • A clear angle + persona shortlist for S39 backed by spend, plus the first real demo read (men 35–54, women 45–54 probes vs the age/gender export).
  • Zero compliance-risk copy on paid traffic — gut-health page dead, gut lines stripped, revival pool scanned.
  • Guardrail: subscription attach stays ~93%. The sub engine adds +0.20 cmROAS in rebills and is why the account runs ~0.74 all-in, not 0.54. Any fix that closes more carts but kills attach is a net loss. cmROAS ranks everything; cart/close/hook rates only say where to look.
